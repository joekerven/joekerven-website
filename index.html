Marine scientist.
Surf instructor.
Poet. Adventurer.

I graduated with a First Class degree in Conservation Biology & Ecology from the University of Exeter. I've helped establish Indonesia's first shark nursery, restored coral reefs in the Gili Islands, surveyed seagrass meadows in Croatia, and filmed some of the UK's rarest marine wildlife — one video hit 213K views.

I've been a Head Surf Instructor in Cornwall for five years, a ski instructor in the Canadian Rockies, and I spent last summer living in a van on the coast. Right now I'm headed to Peru as a rainforest guide. The wild leads. I follow.

I also write — copy, captions, and poetry that makes a reader live the moment. Scientist. Storyteller. Somewhere in the ocean. This is the home base.

The future isn't fixed in stone.
It simply hasn't been written yet.

What changes your future
is what you do now.

Make yourself magnificent
in every tiny thing you do.

Shower with gusto.
Spread your butter with elegance.

Dance in that kitchen
with such ferocity
the house threatens
to dance right alongside you.

Keep your eyes on the stars, fool.
You're shining too bright
to be dimmed by clouds.

Be incandescent.
